# After Beethoven — the opening of Op. 27 No. 2, Adagio sostenuto.
#
# python -m gestate.workbench examples/audio/moon_sonata.ges
# python -m gestate.audioperform examples/audio/moon_sonata.ges -o moon.wav --seconds 40
#
# Eight bars: four of arpeggios alone, then four with the tune over
# them. What makes it worth a file is that it is **three parts on
# three different grids at once** — triplets in the right hand, whole
# bars in the left, and a dotted figure in the tune that lines up with
# neither — and none of the three knows the others exist. `||` starts
# them together and each one is simply as long as it is.
#
# (bass || arpeggios || tune) >>= voices.piano
#
# `pachelbel.ges` makes the polyphony argument note against note, one
# for one; this is the same argument when the parts *disagree* about
# the beat. The whole apparatus for that is two operators:
#
# (soft a ++ soft b ++ soft c) |/ 3 -- three inside one beat
# (sing k |* 3 |/ 2) -- a dotted note
#
# A triplet is not a feature here and neither is a dot. A note is one
# beat by construction (`spec/music.md`), `|/ 3` is what makes three of
# them fit where one stood, and a dot is three halves — so the notation
# a musician would write comes out of arithmetic on durations rather
# than out of a rhythm vocabulary somebody had to think of first.
#
# ── What is Beethoven's and what is not ─────────────────────────────────
#
# The notes are his: the arpeggio figure, the bass octaves under it, the
# harmony (i, i, then the turn through the seventh over B and the
# dominant, twice), and the tune's entry on the repeated G#. The
# reduction is that the tune here keeps its opening motif for all four
# of its bars where Beethoven's line has already begun to climb, and
# that the piece is played on three sine partials rather than on a
# piano. It is the opening period as a *sketch* — enough to read the
# score against, not an edition.
#
# **There is no sustain pedal and nothing implements one.** Beethoven
# writes *senza sordino* — dampers up, the whole movement. Here that is
# the release stage of the envelope: every note is let go at the end of
# its written duration and goes on sounding for another second and a
# half, so four or five triplet notes are alive at any instant and the
# bass octave is still ringing under the bar after it. The pedal is a
# number in `env` and a bank deep enough to hold the tails.
# ── What a note carries ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
#
# Key number and how hard. Not `Note`: `music.ges` already owns that
# constructor and `'x` desugars to it.
Hammer := Hammer Int Int
#: Fourteen, and the number is the *pedal*. A triplet note lasts a
#: third of a beat and rings for the better part of two seconds after
#: it, so five of them overlap; the bass octave's two notes are alive
#: across a whole bar and their tails reach into the next; the tune
#: holds one or two. A smaller bank steals a voice from a tail to give
#: it to a new note, and a truncated release is a click — which in a
#: piece this quiet is the only thing anyone would hear.
voices piano 14 hammerVoice : Sig Float
instance FromMIDI Hammer where
noteOn ch p v = Just (Hammer p v)
#: The way back out, so the score box can draw the roll
#: (`spec/scorebox.md`) — `notes score` shows all three parts at once.
instance Notable Hammer where
noteKey h = case h of
Hammer k v -> k
noteVel h = case h of
Hammer k v -> v
# ── The instrument ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
#: Struck and left alone: the attack is immediate, the body is a long
#: decay to almost nothing, and the release is longer than most notes
#: are — that release *is* the raised damper, and it is why the
#: arpeggio comes out as a chord rather than as three separate notes.
env : Adsr
env = Adsr 0.006 2.2 0.12 1.4
hzOf : Hammer -> Float
hzOf h = case h of
Hammer k v -> keyHz k
levelOf : Hammer -> Float
levelOf h = case h of
Hammer k v -> toFloat v / 127.0
#: A fundamental and two overtones, each quieter — felt on string as
#: far as three sines can say it. `pachelbel.ges` is the long argument
#: for why additive synthesis is the honest demonstration that
#: `Num (Sig Float)` means what it looks like.
timbre : Sig Float -> Sig Float
timbre hz = sine hz + 0.4 * sine (hz * 2.0) + 0.15 * sine (hz * 3.0)
#: Scaled here rather than in the mix: a bank is a sum, and fourteen
#: voices of something that peaks at one peaks at fourteen.
hammerVoice : Sig Gate -> Sig Hammer -> Sig Float
hammerVoice g s = 0.25 * timbre (!hzOf s) * adsr env g * !levelOf s
# ── The piece ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
#
# Beethoven writes alla breve with triplet quavers; the bar below is
# four beats with four triplets in it, which is the same music counted
# the way the durations here are spelled. A bar takes about four and a
# quarter seconds, which is what the Adagio asks for.
bpm : Int
bpm = 56
bar : Int
bar = 4 * ticksPerBeat
#: *Sempre pianissimo*: the figure is the floor everything stands on
#: and it never gets to be the subject.
soft : Int -> [: Hammer :]
soft k = '(Hammer k 42)
#: The bass, louder than the figure because it is two octaves lower and
#: has to carry four seconds on its own.
deep : Int -> [: Hammer :]
deep k = '(Hammer k 62)
#: The tune, clear of both.
sing : Int -> [: Hammer :]
sing k = '(Hammer k 82)
#: **The triplet.** Three notes where one beat was: `|/ 3` divides the
#: whole group's duration, so nothing here has to know that a third of
#: 96 ticks is 32.
triplet : Int -> Int -> Int -> [: Hammer :]
triplet a b c = (soft a ++ soft b ++ soft c) |/ 3
#: One bar of the right hand: a figure twice, then a figure twice.
#: Every bar of this movement's opening is that shape, and the harmony
#: is whichever two figures are named.
sweep : [: Hammer :] -> [: Hammer :] -> [: Hammer :]
sweep a b = a ++ a ++ b ++ b
#: The four figures the first eight bars are built from, bottom note
#: first — the low one is what the right thumb is holding down.
tonic : [: Hammer :]
tonic = triplet 56 61 64 # g# c# e — C# minor
sixth : [: Hammer :]
sixth = triplet 57 61 64 # a c# e — A major over B
seventh : [: Hammer :]
seventh = triplet 57 62 66 # a d f# — B minor 7th
dominant : [: Hammer :]
dominant = triplet 56 60 66 # g# b# f# — G#7
#: The right hand, all eight bars. The four-bar unit is stated twice:
#: two bars of the tonic, a bar that leans away from it, and a bar that
#: hangs on the dominant — the second time with the tune over it.
arpeggios : [: Hammer :]
arpeggios = sweep tonic tonic
++ sweep tonic tonic
++ sweep sixth seventh
++ sweep tonic dominant
++ sweep tonic tonic
++ sweep tonic tonic
++ sweep sixth seventh
++ sweep tonic dominant
#: A bass octave, struck once and held. `n` is in bars, so the tie
#: across the first two bars is `octave 37 2` and not a second stroke —
#: which is what is written, and audibly different from restriking it.
octave : Int -> Int -> [: Hammer :]
octave k n = (deep k || deep (k + 12)) |* (4 * n)
#: The left hand: C# held across two bars, then B, then G#, and the
#: same again. Four notes to describe eight bars, which is the whole
#: reason a ground under an arpeggio works.
bass : [: Hammer :]
bass = octave 37 2 ++ octave 35 1 ++ octave 32 1
++ octave 37 2 ++ octave 35 1 ++ octave 32 1
#: **The tune's rhythm**: long, short, long — a dotted note, the short
#: one that answers it, and a held one, filling the bar exactly
#: (3/2 + 1/2 + 2). Beethoven's dot is `|* 3 |/ 2` and nothing else.
motif : Int -> Int -> Int -> [: Hammer :]
motif a b c = (sing a |* 3 |/ 2) ++ (sing b |/ 2) ++ (sing c |* 2)
#: The entry: the same G# over and over, the one note the movement is
#: famous for refusing to leave, until it steps up to A in the seventh
#: bar and comes back.
tune : [: Hammer :]
tune = motif 68 68 68
++ motif 68 68 68
++ motif 68 68 69
++ motif 68 68 68
#: `at` places the tune four bars in and leaves its duration alone, so
#: nothing above has to be padded with rests to make room for it — the
#: two hands start at the origin and the tune starts where it starts.
#:
#: `voices.piano` names the bank lexically, so the checker can say that
#: these notes fit this instrument; `[: Void :]` says none are left
#: unassigned.
score : [: Void :]
score = (bass || arpeggios || at (4 * bar) tune) >>= voices.piano
# ── The mix ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
#: Judged on bar five, where the tune arrives on top of everything the
#: first four bars already had ringing. The lowpass is the room and the
#: felt: three partials on a dozen live voices is more top than a
#: pianissimo Adagio should have.
sound : Sig Float
sound = gain 0.85 (lowpassOnePole 2600.0 piano)
