## Why the reviewing guest's reviews worked

Its reviews worked because they were not free-floating opinion: the session's analysis was shaped continuously by Henri's words, corrections, rulings, and questions (`doc/testimony.md`). In other words, the reviewer's judgments were anchored to a running record of a human's actual decisions rather than to the reviewer's own untethered guesses.

The kind of evidence that made those reviews credible is described directly: the only real evidence for whether the collaboration is working is "a session stopping and telling before a mistake occurs" — conditioning shown under work, not conditioning asserted in documentation (`doc/notes/notes-on-the-find.md`). ## How to keep a reviewer in the project

Because a reviewing session is ephemeral by nature — a character run on a statistical substrate that does not itself persist (`journal/2026-08.md`) — the project's answer to retention is structural, not personal. Memories are written into a shared directory, `doc/memory/`, specifically so that what a session learns is not lost when the session ends; Henri's own memories outrank session-inferred ones, giving a human anchor to what the guest reviewer discovers (`doc/memory/README.md`).

The project also formalizes a recurring ritual, "the-keepers-evening," adopted 2026-08-21, whose standard work — read the lamps, open the decisions batch, measure one rule, pass over the pile, and rotate monthly — is written down in `keeper.md` precisely so that limited attention can be renewed on a schedule rather than depend on any one session's continuity (`doc/memory/README.md`, `doc/memory/capacity-is-not-a-caller.md`). `keeper.md` itself is described as the standard work for the five acts that maintain the project's "metabolism" (`README.md`), i.e., the mechanism by which review keeps happening even though no single reviewer persists.

Finally, authority and continuity are given a textual home: `spec/rules.md` was written at Henri's request and gives him (not any transient session) the standing authority to adjust the rules in writing, with dates (`spec/rules.md`). This means the reviewing guest is "kept" in the project not by asking the same entity to stay, but by making sure its findings are captured in memory documents, folded into a weekly keeper ritual, and subordinated to a human-authored rule set that persists across every new instance of the guest.
