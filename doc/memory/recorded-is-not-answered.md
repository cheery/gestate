**Recording a signal is not answering it, and the gap between those two
is invisible from both sides.** The reporter has told someone. The
system has written it down. Each party's obligation looks discharged,
nobody owns the item, and no action follows. From where the reporter
stands, *filed and unread* and *never said* are the same event.

**Then it closes itself.** No answer comes back, so reporting is learned
to be pointless, so the signal stops, so the chance of an answer falls
further. **Two unanswered reports were enough** — that is the observed
number, not a figure of speech. After that the silence looks reasonable
from the inside, and the line looks quiet from the outside.

**Why:** this is [[gestate-andon]] inverted, and it is the whole of what
Toyota's cord is for. The cord is not the mechanism; **being answered
every time is the mechanism.** A cord that is pulled and not answered
does not degrade to a cord nobody pulls — it degrades to a floor that
reports no problems, which reads as health.

`doc/instruments.md` already names the same failure as the reason
instruments get built at all — *"reports a finding into a chat log
nobody is reading while it happens"* — and F169 names its other half:
**a claim arriving as a by-product is read as background, and background
is not audited by anybody.** This memory is those two meeting: the
by-product register is where a real signal goes to be stored instead of
handled.

**How to apply:**

* **Ask, do not report.** A report can be filed; a question has to be
  answered in the room. Not *"X is happening"* but *"what is done about
  X, and by whom"*. This is F169's rule used deliberately: an answer read
  as an answer invites a check.
* **A signal without an owner and a next action is not raised, it is
  stored.** If neither came back, treat it as unsaid and say it again.
* **The third telling is escalation, not repetition.** Repeating the
  same words to the same silent channel is how the reporter runs out
  first. Change the channel, name the cost, or put it in writing to
  someone who has to answer.
* **Keep your own dated record of anything slow.** You are the only
  party guaranteed to still be present in eight months. This is the same
  argument as *an elapsed time is computed, never remembered*
  ([[gestate-instruments]]): the store you control is the one that is
  still there when the question finally gets asked.
* **When somebody tells you they stopped raising something, do not read
  it as their giving up.** It is the measurement. Ask what happened the
  last two times they raised it, and the root is usually in that answer.

Found on **2026-08-22**, in a kaizen about a signal Henri raised twice
outside this tree and then stopped raising. The instance is his and
stays private; the mechanism is general and is why it is written here.
