**Henri, at the kaizen of 2026-08-24, after the first deck shipped the
same day it was started:** *"teaching-material should keep that form of
review."*

The form, as it actually ran on `doc/teaching/keepers-first-week.md`:

* **He reviews by notes file** — `~/muistiinpanot.txt`, terse lines,
  batched; the session goes and looks rather than asking what each
  line means, and every note lands as one concrete slide change.
* **A mid-work extra line is not a defect.**  One arrived during the
  edit (the safety rule); priced at one insert and a renumber, and the
  batch it fell out of was the discipline working.
* **Release is his verify, said in the room** — the status line then
  changes to released with the date, said out loud in the reply.
* **The deck keeps its own dated changelog** of a review round — his
  ask, and it stays in the released file.
* **Slides render with `tools/slides.py`** — edit the `.md`,
  regenerate the sibling `.html`; the output stamps its source and
  commit.

**Why:** the first deck went from nothing to released in one sitting
under this loop, and he named the form as the part to keep — not the
deck.  The audience rule travels with it:
[[deriving-strips-the-payment]] — the paying stays in the Speak notes,
never abstracted onto the slide.

**How to apply:** week two (`card:teaching-week-two.md`) and the later
domains start inside this loop: draft in `doc/teaching/`, point him at
it, read his notes file, land each note, wait for his verify.  Related:
[[the-kaizen-is-asked-not-answered]], [[lead-with-the-noun]],
[[henri-working-style]].
