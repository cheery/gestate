From 2026-08-17 Henri works the board **away from the desk** — available,
not present ("I am here for you in need. Available but just relax as much
as I can."). The channel back to him is a sound: `tools/andon.sh` (ring
once) or `tools/andon.sh 3` (three rings, eight seconds apart), playing
`tools/andon.ges` — three rising sine chimes, ~2 s, soft attack. Built
and verified by him the morning of 2026-08-17: *"It works."*

**Why:** unattended sessions had no way to ask anything, and the sound
card was already the one channel from a running session into his room —
he noticed it himself when a headless test run played `noted.ges` aloud.

**How to apply:** ring for a decision that is expensive to get wrong and
cheap to ask about, and batch the questions first — the board's own rule
is one sitting, not one interruption per card ([[gestate-next-session]]).
The three-ring cap is deliberate: more rings only leave noise waiting for
him. Default when he is away is to keep going and write a blocked card up
as blocked. Do not build a second andon phrase for a second meaning
without asking — one cord, one meaning.
