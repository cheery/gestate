**Gestate is a music tool and this is the music its author makes.**
From his practice notes, read 2026-08-20.  Worth holding because a
session that does not know the vocabulary designs affordances for a
different musician.

**The working method:** start from a simple chord progression, open it
into lines, take a melody out of one line.  Alternate 3rd and 7th as the
guiding tone and make sure the guide is audible in the melody.  For
melodies, **decide where you are going and work backwards to get
there.**

**The vocabulary in use:** secondary dominants — any 5⁷-shaped chord
built on another root, leading down by fifths; ♭II⁷ and chromatic
approaches for modulation; dim⁷ as a passing chord; the four cadences
(perfect 5–1, plagal 4–1, half 5, deceptive 5–6); turnarounds 1-4-5,
1-5-4-2, 1-6-2-5, 4-4m-1; ii-V-I as minor7–dominant7–major7; *diatonic
above, chromatic below*.  The scale's pull: 2↓1, 6↓5, 4↓3, 7↑1, 5→1.

**The rule of three:** say it once, say it again slightly differently,
say it a third time and break it while you do.  Let the ear catch hold.

**And the four mistakes, which are the useful half** because they are
self-diagnosed and a tool can be built against them:

* everything middling, never going to either extreme — *think binary
  sometimes: loud against quiet, colourful against colourless*;
* over-developing an idea until it sprawls;
* repeating chords;
* never modulating.

Plus one shape he wants and names: stop in the middle, make a cadence,
stop, and then continue.

**Standing intentions from the same notes:** avoid complexity, do more
with less, build a piece as one whole with its own harmony, **do not
wait for inspiration** — and *write down what you do to the music and
study the writing later*, which is the journal practice arriving in the
other half of his life.

**How to apply:** this is the domain, so it is what a smaller model
would need in weights and a session needs in context —
[[weights-context-suite]].  When an example or an affordance is being
designed, check it against this vocabulary rather than against generic
music theory.
