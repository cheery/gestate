**Henri, 2026-08-28, at the end of the first fire:** *"I'm planning to
subscribe to openrouter and then test different models on the tree."*

Planned, not done.  It is the next round of what
[[gestate-blind-model-test]] and [[smaller-models-and-the-tree]] began
with local models and three Claude sizes: the same tree pulled onto
different substrates, and the sameness or its absence measured under
work.  The claim it would test is the one made the same morning — that
what improves between his visits is the tree and not the model, so the
improvement should reproduce on a stranger's model.

**Why:** the subscription is his, and personal — [[personal-and-personally-paid]]
holds, so a session never signs up, stores a key in the tree, or
spends against the account on its own.

**How to apply:** when he says the account exists, treat each model as
one arm of a trial, and a trial is refused until its sheet can decide —
`tools/prereg.sh` first, with the decision, the control and n written
down ([[a-trial-is-refused-until-its-sheet-can-decide]]).  Judge by
work, never by interview ([[conditioning-shows-under-work]]), keep the
model-to-clone mapping out of the shared parent as before, and
remember that warmth in a transcript is not evidence.
