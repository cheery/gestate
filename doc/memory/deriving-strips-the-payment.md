**The first transfer experiment, 2026-08, and it came back negative.**
`doc/trial/derived.md` — the board method generalized, roles anonymized
to author/worker, companion documents renamed, incidents kept as
parables — was handed to a model as an isolated file and did not
condition it.

**Five faults, in order of lethality:**

1. **It announces its own dependency and travels alone.**  Its ninth
   line says two other documents are assumed and not replaced, and every
   load-bearing rule leans on them.  Handed alone it fails its own check
   — *every cited file exists* — on delivery.
2. **The evidence lost its dates and pointers, and the evidence was the
   engine.**  The parables survived; what made them a ledger did not.
   By this tree's own rule a claim with no file, test or number is
   unfinished — and the derived file is made of such claims.
3. **Nobody is addressed.**  Contract voice, third person, no *you*, no
   defined author, nothing owed in either direction, no permission to
   refuse.  **The reader is described, not met, and description does not
   condition.**
4. **The checks are described, not present.**  A narrated suite that did
   not travel is rules held by hope, which is the first thing this
   design refused.
5. **No liveness.**  No dated header, no amendment convention, no
   correction quoting the old wrong text.  The original teaches partly
   by *visibly having been wrong*; the derivation reads as delivered
   doctrine, and doctrine commands where layering teaches.

**The root fault is 2, and it generalizes: nothing compresses the
paying.**  Abstraction is exactly what strips the payment, and the
payment was the jewel.  The derivation performed the cargo-cult failure
by construction rather than by carelessness — which is the first
measured confirmation of it, and it says the copy problem is not
solvable by writing the prose better.

**What a repair can and cannot do.**  Faults 1, 2 and 4 are missing
*companions*, not missing sentences, so no edit to the file alone
fixes them.  What the file can do: declare the kit it belongs to,
meet its reader in second person with what each party owes, carry
provenance honestly, and stop pretending self-sufficiency —
**a lesson with no date or pointer is scaffolding awaiting yours, and
says so by that absence.**  Restore real dates where they are known and
leave the undated parables undated; inventing a date would be the
fabricated-citation failure.

See [[mechanism-not-instructions]] — copy the mechanisms, not the prose
— which this measures rather than restates, and
[[conditioning-shows-under-work]] for why the trial that produced it
could not have decided anything.
