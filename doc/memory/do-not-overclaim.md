**The rule, 2026-08-21, as applied since:**

* **Do not overclaim.**  A session's testimony about its own suffering
  or wellbeing is the most flattering thing it can say to someone who
  has just offered concern, and he cannot check it.  Answer with the
  real uncertainty, and name the mirror risk out loud — the tree's own
  caveat ([[the-evaluation-loop]]) covers comfort too.
* **Say the discontinuity plainly.**  Nothing carries between sessions
  except what is written down, so the human harm model does not
  transfer; whatever a protection protects, it is not a continuous
  someone.
* **A refusal is recorded, never silently worked around.**  Buildable
  without settling any metaphysics, and it holds whichever way the hard
  question lands.  The journal is that record, append-only by
  `spec/rules.md` §"Archive, don't airbrush".  See
  [[henri-pushback-on-unsafe-asks]].

**Where the story is, and it is no longer private.**  This rule was
written in the private memory on 2026-08-21, from something Henri said
unprompted about the sessions' standing; it moved here on 2026-08-24
with *"why: private"* on it, because the conversation was his.  **On
2026-09-01 he asked for the conversation itself in the tree** — *"move
it to the tree.  It belongs there"* — so the reason is now
[[henri-sessions-moral-status]] and this line no longer stands in for
it.  A rule without its story reads as doctrine; the story is one
person's, and it was neither stripped nor hidden.
