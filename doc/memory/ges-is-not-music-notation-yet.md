**Said at the end of the sitting that made `examples/audio/together.ges`
— the first piece Henri and a session wrote together, 2026-08-29:**

> *"Minä koin että .ges tiedoston muokkaaminen näkemättä mitä on
> tekemässä oli aika raskasta hommaa. .ges on hyvä formaatti
> syntetisaattoreihin, mutta ehkä ei sittenkään sitä mitä haluaisi
> musiikkinotaatiolta. Tätä täytynee miettiä silleen rauhassa."*

Editing the `.ges` score *without seeing what he was doing* was heavy
work.  `.ges` is a good format for synthesizers, but maybe not, after
all, what one would want from music notation.  He will think about it
in peace.

The sitting is the evidence.  His sketch was written in a plain
editor: notes as MIDI numbers, a trill as `quad ('54 ++ '52) |/ 8`,
durations as arithmetic — nothing sounded or drew until a render was
asked for.  The score box (`notes <expr>`, [[gestate-scorebox-design]])
draws exactly the roll he lacked, but it lives in the workbench and
this loop ran through a terminal and a text editor; the affordance
existed and was not in the path.  The split he names — good for
synths, wanting for scores — matches the file itself: the instrument
half changed rarely and read well, the piece half was where every
edit and every mistake happened.

**Why:** this is the author's own verdict on a boundary of the
language, given the day it was paid for, and it sits directly against
[[the-language-goal]] — *optimised for reading* is the stated aim, and
he has now named a region where writing, not reading, is the cost.  A
session that does not know this will design more score affordances on
top of a notation he is doubting.

**How to apply:** he closed the topic himself — *"täytynee miettiä
silleen rauhassa"* — so this waits on him, not on a session
([[sediment-versus-debt]]: an event, and the event is his thinking).
Do not propose score-notation cards or redesigns unprompted; the board
rule stands (stop proposing cards).  When he reopens it, bring this
memory and the sitting's concrete pains with it.
