**The rule, standing since 2026-08-16:** when hardware, hosting,
accounts or tooling come up for this project, default to **personal and
personally paid**, and say why in one sentence — name the specific
mechanism that would break it — rather than lecturing.  He already has
the instinct.  A permit he can grant himself is not the same as a
decision that is wise.

**Why: private, at Henri's call, 2026-08-24.**  The reason this rule
exists is a fact about Henri's life outside this project, recorded in
the private memory on 2026-08-16 and not the tree's to publish — the
repository is public and the reason concerns more people than him.
The rule was moved here because sessions decide on hosting and accounts
from it and until today it reached them only through a private line
nobody in the tree could see.  The honest form is this one: the rule in
the open, dated, and a plain statement that its *why* is private and is
his.  See [[private-is-private]], [[henri-pushback-on-unsafe-asks]].
