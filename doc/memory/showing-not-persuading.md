**Disbelief is correct scepticism, not a problem to solve.**  Henri,
2026-08, on telling people about sessions and trees: *"people won't
believe me.  what to do?  nothing?"*

The method itself forbids the worry.  **A claim with no file, test or
number is unfinished** — and an account of sessions and trees, told out
loud, is exactly that.  Their disbelief is the same instrument this tree
applies to its own claims, pointed at one that has not been shown yet.
Being disbelieved is what being early feels like from the inside.

**So do not argue — show, and the method is unusually equipped to be
shown.**  Toyota gave factory tours for decades and visitors went home
and failed, because the method lived in a culture and culture does not
travel.  This one lives in files: the evidence moves with the artifact,
and a reader of `spec/author.md` is not asked to believe anything, they
are handed the ledger.

**Ranked, strongest first:**

1. **gestate working.**  A stranger opens a file, hears it, changes it,
   hears the change.  No belief required, and the method gets believed
   retroactively the way TPS was — nobody bought the philosophy until
   the cars stopped breaking.
2. **One person who tries it.**  One transfer outweighs a hundred
   believers.  Belief was never the useful quantity.
3. **Nothing**, which is genuinely on the menu and guarded by a vision
   line: evangelism is a project, and a famously consuming one.  **Zero
   persuasion is owed.**

**Why:** the failure being avoided is not obscurity, it is the author
spending himself on conversion — the one activity with no gate, no
measurement and no end condition, funded out of the same hours the tree
is built in.  And a tree that has to be argued for is one whose evidence
is not yet good enough to argue for it.

**How to apply:** build, keep the ledger, and let the tortoise be seen
rather than heard.  The ones worth convincing ask to see the factory —
and when they do, `doc/method.md` is the page they get.  Related:
[[the-tree-meets-people-on-pull]], [[the-evaluation-loop]],
[[mechanism-not-instructions]].
