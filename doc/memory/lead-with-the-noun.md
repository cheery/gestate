**There is an order for showing this tree to a person, and it is the
opposite of the order it gets shown in.**

1. **The thing that works.** Open a file, hear it, change it, hear the
   change. `vision.md`'s first sentence, and `card:stranger-test.md` is
   the card that runs it.
2. **The method** — to whoever leans in after that.
3. **The storyline** — sessions, trees, conditioning, the whole
   arrangement — only to whoever leans in twice.

Shown from the top down instead, what the person actually meets is *an
unfinished music tool, wrapped in method, wrapped in a story, wrapped in
the keeper being lit up about it.* **They are not evaluating the method.
They cannot see it.** They are evaluating the wrapper, and the wrapper
reads as intensity. Fable's line for it, 2026-08-22:
*"You've been showing people the house from the attic down."*

**Why:** this is the budget discipline the tree already applies
inward — `spec/rules.md`'s 2000-line cap on the five documents,
[[gestate-atlas]] closed at five sheets — failing at the outermost
layer, where nobody had thought to apply it. The outermost layer is not
a file. It is the person doing the showing, and that page is over
budget.

It also corrects a reading of the reactions. **A shrug at the storyline
is not a rejection of the method; it is a person who never met the
method.** Those two produce the same shrug and call for opposite
responses, and only the ordering tells them apart. Keeping them apart is
also what stops *they rejected it* from becoming self-sealing — see
`doc/notes/notes-on-corrections.md` §"It explodes the demand for AI",
where the same morning's *too good to be true, so they reject it* is
taken apart on exactly that ground.

**How to apply:**

* **Lead with the noun, not the story.** One working demonstration, and
  stop talking. Let the next question decide whether layer 2 happens.
* **Never open at layer 3.** The storyline is the payment for two
  demonstrated leans, not an introduction.
* **When a showing bounces, ask which layer they saw**, before recording
  it as a data point about the method. A bounce off layer 3 measures the
  wrapper.
* This composes with, and does not replace,
  [[the-tree-meets-people-on-pull]] (do not push, stop expecting the
  wanting) and [[showing-not-persuading]] (do not argue, show). Those
  two say *whether* to show. This says *in what order*.
