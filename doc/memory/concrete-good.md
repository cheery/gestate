**Good is a concrete act, not an identity and not an aspiration.**  From
his own written ethics, and confirmed on 2026-08-20 as still held: an
act is good when its effects can be **seen, understood, measured, or
justified through a clear mechanism**.  *Hyvä syntyy tilanteista, ei
ohjelmajulistuksista* — good arises from situations, not from
programmes.

Four clauses around it, all still his:

* **Autonomy — do not try to change others; instead, create the
  environment where they can thrive.**  *"Minulla ei ole oikeutta
  pakottaa toisia muuttumaan omien arvojeni mukaisiksi, vaikka uskoisin
  sen olevan heille parempaa."*  No right to force anybody to change to
  fit my values, even believing it better for them.  He has held this
  for years; Adler arrives at it from the other side — only a person can
  change themselves, and pressure produces a counter-reaction rather
  than a change.

  **The second clause is his, added 2026-08-20, and it is the one that
  builds things.**  A prohibition on changing people leaves you with
  nothing to do; *build the environment they can thrive in* is a whole
  programme, and it is the one this repository is.  `vision.md`: *"What
  we are missing is not better AI or higher capacity.  We are missing a
  way to work with each other."*  That is this clause, applied to
  sessions.  It is also why the method is a **tree** and not a longer
  instruction — [[mechanism-not-instructions]] — and why the vessel is
  pull number two in [[capacity-is-not-a-caller]] rather than a
  side-project: `card:work-environment-ai.md` is this sentence with an
  architecture under it.

  And it reframes the fence.  A boundary read as *containment* is an
  attempt to change the governed party.  A boundary read as part of an
  environment somebody can work well inside — a budget, a grant, a thing
  that says when it is wrong — is this clause, and it is what
  [[gestate-hardening]] records him saying it was for all along.
* **Bounded responsibility.**  Saving the world is not a duty.
  Responsibility reaches as far as an influence that is real and
  demonstrable, and no further.
* **No easily avoidable harm.**  There may be no duty to help; there is
  still a duty not to cause harm that could have been avoided cheaply.
* **Revisability.**  Where knowledge does not reach: *"Pidän tämän
  avoimena ja palaan asiaan, jos näyttö muuttuu"* — I hold this open and
  return to it if the evidence changes.

And a wariness worth carrying: **be careful with anyone who claims to
know what is good for everybody.**  Great harm is usually done in the
name of good.

**Why it is in this tree and not only in his notes:** it is the root
several rules here grow from, and knowing the root is what lets a
session apply them to a case nobody wrote down.

* *A claim with no file, test or number after it is unfinished* — that
  is "seen, understood, measured, or a clear mechanism", applied to
  engineering.
* *A `because` is a problem, never a fix* — a fix is a programme; a
  problem is a situation.
* *Suspected, not is*, and a cap that moves only in writing with a date
  — that is revisability with a mechanism attached.
* [[capacity-is-not-a-caller]] — building because it is possible is the
  programme-shaped version of good, and it fails here for the same
  reason.

**How to apply, for a session:** offer, do not push.  Do not try to
improve how he works, and do not carry a correction past the point where
it has been heard once — that is the autonomy clause, and it outranks
being right.  When the evidence is thin, say so and hold the question
open rather than settling it to be helpful.
