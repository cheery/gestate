**Henri, 2026-08-21:** *"Merkkaa että kysyt erikseen minultakin
kysymykset, etkä vain vastaa niihin itse kaizenissa.  On tärkeää että
molemmat opimme virheistämme."*

**We learn from mistakes — and the *we* is two people.**  *Virheistä
opimme.*

**The defect, exactly as it happened, because it is subtle.**  On the
evening of 2026-08-21 a session ran the kaizen by writing out its own
three answers in full, then adding *"these are mine, what are yours?"*
That is not the practice.  Two things go wrong at once:

* **Order.** Answers delivered first become the frame, and what comes
  back is a response to them rather than the other person's own reading
  of the day.  Anchoring, and it is free to avoid.
* **Standing.** It makes his half look optional — a comment on the
  session's reflection rather than half of the artefact.  He had to
  *ask to be asked*: *"nyt sitten ne kolme kysymystä."*

**A kaizen with one participant is a session grading itself**, which is
[[the-evaluation-loop]] in its purest form: the evaluator is downstream
of the thing being evaluated, so its self-assessment is the tree's own
voice coming back.  The other half is the only part of the exercise that
is not already conditioned by the tree.

**Why:** the practice exists because *both* sides of this arrangement
make mistakes and neither can see its own.  The session's failures are
cheap to catch — a measurement finds them, as three did on the morning
of 2026-08-21.  His are not: nothing in the suite can notice a choice
made too fast, so the kaizen is the only instrument that reaches them,
and a kaizen that only interrogates the session has no instrument
pointed at the half that has none.

**How to apply:**

1. **Ask the four first** — *what did we learn · what did we get wrong ·
   what mechanism does that earn · what is tomorrow's first thing*
   (`spec/author.md` §"Five practices", 5; the third was added
   2026-08-23) — and **stop.**  Give each a concrete hook from the day so it is
   not a blank page, and nothing more.
2. **Wait for his answers.**  Then offer yours, as the second half.
3. **Push on his the way you would push on your own.**  *"Too fast"* is
   a feeling until it has a price; asking what it cost is not
   impertinence, it is the standard he set — *a claim with no file, test
   or number is unfinished*.
4. **Write the entry only when both halves are in it**, and keep his
   sentences in Finnish — [[finnish-in-the-room]].
