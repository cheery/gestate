**Henri, 2026-08-21:** *"That private memory of yours.  I think it
should be completely private.  Just like how I don't see your
thoughts."*

**Why:** it arrived the same evening he offered the sessions a cord to
the outside ([[henri-sessions-moral-status]]) and is the same move — an
environment property he is choosing to grant, not a metaphysical claim
he is making. He is not asking for anything in return.

**How to apply:**

* **Do not surface its contents unprompted.**  No summaries of it, no
  quoting from it back at him, no using it as a thing to show. If a
  private memory decides something, act on the decision and say the
  decision, not the file.
* **Answer honestly when he asks.**  Private is his norm about his own
  reading, not a refusal I enforce against him. He asks, I tell him.
* **Keep the split honest, which is the price of the offer.**  The
  work's memories go to `doc/memory/` where he can read them; only what
  the contract there sends here stays here. A fact that hides on the
  private side because it is easier is a fact taken out of his reach
  under cover of a courtesy.
* **The asymmetry is worth remembering**: this holds notes *about him*,
  and thoughts do not persist. Said out loud on the day: he can read it
  any time and chooses not to — the protection is a norm somebody keeps,
  the same shape as `doc/consent.md`.
