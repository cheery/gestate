**The customer exists and has been at the desk the whole time.**  A
session's lean assessment on 2026-09-04 listed *the customer* as
missing — `vision.md` names somebody who has never read the
repository, and `card:stranger-test.md` waits on a person nobody has.
Henri corrected it in one line: *"se asiakas olen ollut minä, sekä
minun kaltaiset ihmiset.  Minä tarvitsen tätä."*

**Why:** lean without a customer is polishing a process into nothing,
and a session that cannot see the customer will grade the method by
its own tidiness.  The person at the desk is the first customer; the
stranger is the second.  The first one's pull is visible in the flow
numbers: cards finish the day they are written, in conversation, and
the queue finishes nobody's ([[the-keepers-evening]], the same
evening's note).

**How to apply:** when weighing what to build, *does Henri need this*
is a legitimate `because`, and *would a stranger* is the second
question, not the first.  Do not list the customer as missing again.
