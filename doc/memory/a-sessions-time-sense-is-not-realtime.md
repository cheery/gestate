**A session's time sense differs from real time a lot** — Henri's words,
2026-08-27, after a session read a sweep batch and a platform 6 visit on
the same Friday as a collision and offered to let the batch slide:

> you run the batch in minutes.  And the platform 6 meet is just few
> hours.  Morning has plenty of time to keep a session.  Remember that
> your time sense differs from realtime a lot.

**Why:** a session lives in turns, not hours.  A task that fills a
session's whole context reads as *large*, and a person's outing that
takes no turns at all reads as *a day gone* — neither is measured
against a clock.  So a session over-weights its own work in the day and
under-weights how much of a person's day is left around it, and the
error shows up as scheduling advice: batches moved, reviews slid,
collisions announced that nobody at the desk would see.

**How to apply:** before saying two things collide, put a wall-clock
number on each — minutes for the machine (`git log` shows what a batch
cost; the suite says twelve seconds for the gates and 25 minutes for the
full pass), hours for the person, as he gives them.  A dated task and an
outing in one day are not a conflict until the numbers say so, and the
default when they do not is silence: the schedule on
`card:ungated-fixes.md` already says what a missed day does, and it is
his to invoke, not a session's to predict.  The same error runs the
other way — see [[a-sitting-is-a-body-constraint]]: a session's sense
that *there is time left* is no measure of his either.
