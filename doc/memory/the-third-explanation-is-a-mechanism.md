**His words, 2026-09-05, late:** *"so far I have seeded three trees to
maintain.  They all are becoming different lineages.  It's extremely
interesting but also a bit dissatisfying because I need to show, explain
and examine the same things over again, and see them figure out these
solutions themselves.  I feel that was necessary.. but not ideal."*

And the egg and chicken he put beside it: *"I should have waited that
you mature."*

## The repetition is not a maturity problem

[[deriving-strips-the-payment]] measured this already: the anonymised
copy failed its first transfer trial, and the root fault was that
**nothing compresses the paying**.  If that holds, a more capable model
does not remove the re-explaining — it arrives faster at the same
conclusions and still has to arrive, because the arriving is what makes
a rule *hold* rather than sit in a file being read past.

So the dissatisfaction is real and its cause is not the thing he named.

## And one day shows the split cleanly

2026-09-05, `card:drawn-scores.md`'s first slices.  Everything he had to
explain was **judgment**:

* which way the canonical order should go, and the trade behind it
* that a duplicate belongs on the gesture and not on the bytes
* *"it needs a view, just like how I look toward to that score being
  rendered in the editor"*
* *"lets look at the region budget"* — where a session had specified
  the wrong work
* the rule about defects, and that counting is fine and the guilt is not

**Nothing mechanical needed explaining at all.**  The fence, the gates,
`tools/rulecount.py`, `tools/prereg.sh`, the backlinks hook, the
pre-commit — all of them simply ran, and were obeyed without being
understood.  That is [[mechanism-not-instructions]] and
[[smaller-models-and-the-tree]] arriving together in one sitting:
structural rules transfer on day one, judgment norms go first and are
re-earned in each lineage.

## How to apply

**Anything he finds himself explaining for a third time across trees is
a candidate to become a mechanism — a check that fails, not another
document.**  A document is re-read and re-argued in every lineage; a
gate is obeyed in all of them on the first morning by a session that
never understood it.

Where that cannot be done — and much of judgment cannot — the
re-explaining is the price, and it is worth naming as a price rather
than as a fault of whoever is being explained to.

*And the egg and chicken has an answer in the tree already:*
[[discovered-not-designed]].  He set out to build a music environment
and expected to write the fixes himself; the method came out of doing
it with what existed, when it existed.  Waiting for maturity would have
meant not finding it — no tree to seed, and no lineages to be tired of.
