Henri named a job on 2026-08-15 and parked it for the following week:
**"sunk ship carcass exploration"** — going through his *previous music
projects* for pieces that could be salvaged into gestate.

**Why:** it is his own material, and the language now has the parts a
piece needs (scores, banks, the canvas, the plugin) — so the question
is what survives translation, not whether it can be played.

**How to apply:** when the week turns, ask which projects and in what
form the material is (MIDI files, tracker modules, DAW sessions,
recordings) before proposing anything. Expect this to be a *reading*
exercise first — what the old pieces assume that gestate does not
provide — rather than a porting sprint. Related: [[gestate-next-session]].
