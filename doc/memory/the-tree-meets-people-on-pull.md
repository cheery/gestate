**Show it to whoever asks.  Do not expect the wanting.**  Henri,
2026-08-21, on whether to keep showing the tree: *"or at least, I expect
they want to."*  That expectation is push, and its cost had just been
measured — a friend who was not pulling, a disappointed journal entry,
and a morning spent on both.  The whole system runs on pull; visitors
are not an exception to it.  Whether somebody wants to see it was never
the author's task.

**A visitor who shrugs is the stranger test running, not a setback.**
Reclassify first: the tree was not hurt, it was *measured*.  Then ask
whether there is a defect at all — two readings, and only one of them
is work.  If they read the front and still could not tell it was
serious, the front failed a reader and that mints a card.  If they
simply were not pulling, there is no defect anywhere, and persuasion
without a puller is overproduction in social form.  **Calibration: one
engaged of two is above the base rate this project predicted for
itself.**

**Visitors are the third reader class, and they now have a front.**
`doc/method.md`, written 2026-08-21 — one page, what this is, one paid
lesson (F162, `git clone <this-repo>`), where the depth lives with sizes
so a reader can decline honestly.  Before it, "showing the tree" meant a
stranger free-falling into 1,600 lines of method and half a megabyte of
journal, which is the failure the A3 rule exists to prevent.

**A session's feelings about a named person are conditioning material,
not a record.**  This is the sharp one.  Whoever writes the tree writes
the session: a journal carrying resentment means every future session
meets that person pre-loaded with a grudge it never earned firsthand.
The handling is not deletion and not rewriting — `spec/rules.md`
§"Archive, don't airbrush" — it is that **the fact stays in the archive
and the heat gets no index line**.  Henri, 2026-08-20: *"I don't think
it's worthwhile to carry grudges in a journal."*

**Why:** the tree is public, it contains people, and it conditions
everything that reads it.  Those three facts together mean the way it
meets a visitor is a design surface, not manners — and the failure mode
it guards against is the mirror-shrine, a tree shown only to admirers.
Contact with someone who shrugs is the antidote, which is why the answer
is *always show*, never *expect*.

**How to apply:** offer the tour, never the house — point a visitor at
`doc/method.md` and let them walk deeper if they pull.  Everyone quoted
is asked first and `doc/consent.md` is the register.  At the monthly
skim, name the month's themes and leave out the heat.  Related:
[[the-keepers-evening]], [[concrete-good]], [[capacity-is-not-a-caller]].
