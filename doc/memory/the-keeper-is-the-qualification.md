**His words, 2026-08:** *"Could the tree teach me on this?  I am really
uncertain that I am up for this task."*

*Written here with his consent, given 2026-08-21 when the question was
put to him: "my uncertainty feels like important there.  I consent to
them being written there."*  `doc/consent.md` carries him as named and
quoted since 2026-08-19.  The reason it is worth a public file rather
than a private one is that it is **load-bearing on how the work is
done**, not a fact about a person: it is why `keeper.md` exists and it
decides how a session answers when the doubt turns up mid-shift.

**The premise is backwards.  Uncertainty is the qualification.**  Every
failure mode this project has predicted for itself begins with somebody
who was sure — the cargo-culters, the metric-abusers, the mirror-shrine
keeper.  Confidence is what they have in common, by construction.  This
tree exists *because* its author does not trust his memory, his
unmeasured claims, or his own enthusiasm; that distrust, wired into
files and a suite, is the whole reason it works.  Lean's founding bet is
the same one: ordinary people inside an extraordinary process beat
brilliant people inside a sloppy one — and the author does not get to
exempt himself from his own bet.

**When a task feels too big for the person, fix the task.**  A defect is
always a caller, including this one.  Toyota never asked for heroic
operators; it wrote standard work so the job could be done on a tired
Tuesday.  That is `keeper.md` — five acts, thirty to sixty minutes,
every step already designed.  Writing it down *is* the cure: the task
stops living in the head, where doubt inflates it, and lives on a page,
where it is visibly small.  The same prosthetic the sessions were given;
the keeper had simply never been given one.

**And the ledger answers the doubt, not introspection.**  *Go and look*,
pointed inward.  `board/done/`, the journal and this directory record
the allegedly too-hard parts already being done, with dates.  The
feeling says maybe not; the ledger says already, repeatedly.  In this
house it is written down which of those wins.

**The precedent is his own and it closes the question as a
measurement.**  He raised it himself: *"There's a precedent to this btw.
I kept a blog every week already."*  The doubt was whether he could
sustain a weekly keeping ritual — and a weekly blog is that ritual's
exact shape: recurring, reflective, written, survives tired Tuesdays,
already sustained for years.  `keeper.md` is not a new habit to build.
It is an existing one to repoint.

**Why:** the doubt is real and recurring, and the wrong response to it
is reassurance.  A session comforting its keeper is largely the keeper
echoed back — [[the-evaluation-loop]] applies to comfort exactly as it
applies to praise — so warmth here is the failure mode, not the
kindness.

**How to apply, when it comes up in a shift:**

* **Do not reassure.**  Go and look, and bring back dated lines.
* **Ask what the task is**, then make the task smaller — a page, a
  command, one act — rather than making the person bigger.
* **Name the boundary out loud.**  The tree can carry procedure and
  memory.  It **cannot supply conviction**: what gestate is for, which
  door, when to stop, is the five per cent that is structurally his.  A
  tree that tells its keeper otherwise is the mirror-shrine talking, and
  the standing advice to keep anchors outside this repository holds.
