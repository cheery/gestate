**Henri, 2026-08-23, at the kaizen:** *"Minä opin että helpottaa paljon
jos on tietoa saatavilla päätöksentekoon.  On hyvä ja helppo kysyä
tutkimusta päätöksenteon avuksi.  Ongelmatonta se ei ole ja asiat täytyy
silti lukea huolella, mutta tieto auttaa tekemään hyviä päätöksiä."*

And, on the rule that keeps the habit safe: *"'pyydä tutkimusta joka
jättää jälkeensä komennon' on oikea järjestys."*

**Two things come back from a session asked to research a decision, and
they are not the same thing.**

* **A measurement carries a command.**  `git show
  5f42f68:board/README.md`, `tools/dangling.py --at 5f42f68`,
  `python tools/rulecount.py`.  You can run it, and it answers the same
  way whoever asks.  That is what makes it evidence.
* **A recommendation carries nothing.**  It is a ranking, and a session's
  ranking is a conditioned voice — *sessions are inside the
  conditioning*, `doc/notes/notes-on-drift.md`.  It can be right, and it
  was on the day this was written, and that changes nothing about what
  it is.

**Why:** the habit of asking for research is good and it routes a
decision through the one thing that cannot check the keeper.  The line
between the two halves is what keeps it safe, and it is cheap to hold —
**ask for research that leaves a command behind**, then read the command's
answer rather than the paragraph around it.

**How to apply:**

1. **When asked to investigate a decision, hand back the commands.**  Not
   *"C is expensive"* but the line that prices it and what it printed.  A
   finding a session cannot spell as a command is a finding the author
   has to take on trust, which is the state this rule exists to leave.
2. **Say which half is which.**  Mark the ranking as suspected and keep
   it separate from the measurement, so accepting one is not accepting
   the other.
3. **Distrust a clean investigation hardest when it agrees with the
   session that ran it.**  On 2026-08-23 the measurement killed the
   investigating session's own proposal — that is the good case and it is
   also the only reason the research counted for anything.
