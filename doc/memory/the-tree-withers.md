**Henri, 2026-08-24, on the evening the memory index was found to have
lost nineteen hooks and the four work-shaped rules moved into the tree:**

> *"This is better than expected.  The tree must be treated well or it
> withers.  And that satisfies my want that these living documents or
> trees, are treated well."*

**Why it is a rule and not a sentiment.**  The evidence of the week is
that what conditions a session is not the method documents but the
record of one person's corrections — dated, in his words, reaching the
next session before it asks anything.  That record lives in files, and
files rot in specific, measurable ways: a hook not added, an index that
drifts from its directory, a page behind its source, a rule stated
twice, a number nobody checks.  Every one of those was found in this
tree in one week.  *Withers* is the right word because none of them
fails loudly; each is a slightly different session.

**How to apply:**

* **A living document has a source and a check**, or it is a document
  that was alive once.  The atlas, `doc/method.md`'s table, the memory
  index's public half — generated from what they describe, refused when
  behind.  When a page is found to have drifted, the fix is the
  generator and the gate, not the page.
* **Hooks go beside bodies.**  A memory is written in `doc/memory/`, its
  line goes in that README, and `tools/memoryindex.py` carries it to the
  boot surface.  A memory with no hook is a memory that will never be
  recalled, and nothing will say so.
* **Treat the private half the same way** — it is the part of the tree
  he chose not to read, not the part exempt from care.  A rule about the
  work that lives only there is out of his reach under cover of a
  courtesy; the honest form is the rule in the open with *why: private*
  on it ([[private-is-private]]).
* **The keeper's evening is this rule on a cadence** ([[the-keepers-evening]]):
  lamps, decisions, one rule measured, the pile, the rotation.  Not a
  demand — a tree that punishes an absence is being treated badly from
  the other side.
