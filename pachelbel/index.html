# Pachelbel's Canon: five parts at once, on an instrument that is a sum.
#
#   python -m gestate.audiopygame examples/audio/pachelbel.ges
#   python -m gestate.audioeditor examples/audio/pachelbel.ges
#   python -m gestate.audioperform examples/audio/pachelbel.ges --seconds 20
#
# **The whole instrument is nine lines and every one of them is a value.**
# That is the example.  `polysine.ges` makes the argument on one sine; this
# is what the argument buys once there is a real piece to play — a timbre
# that is a sum of harmonics, a pitch that is a product with a vibrato, and
# an amplitude that is a product with an envelope, all written as the
# arithmetic they are:
#
#     timbre (!hzOf s * vibrato) * adsr env g * !levelOf s
#
# Nothing here declares a state record, folds one, or takes a note apart
# per sample.  `duet.ges` and `quartet.ges` write their voices the long way
# and are worth reading against this file: they are the same sounds spelled
# as hand-rolled state machines, seventy lines where these are nine.  What
# closed the gap was `sine`/`adsr` in `synth.ges` — the fold
# written *once* — and `instance Num (Sig Float)` in `audio.ges`, which is
# what makes `+` and `*` mean what they look like.  `spec/frp_lesson.md`
# is the reading: in Fran a behaviour is a value, and `stretch (abs wiggle)
# charlotte` applies ordinary functions to something that varies without
# anywhere saying so.
#
# ── Why this piece ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
#
# Because a ground bass is polyphony you can *check*.  Everything above the
# bass is derived from it — the chords are what those eight notes imply and
# the tune is a line through them — so there is nothing in the score that
# is true only because it sounded right, and a wrong note is arithmetic
# rather than taste.  It also puts five notes under the same instant, which
# is the honest test that a `voices` bank is polyphonic: a monophonic synth
# plays a fifth of this file.
#
# `examples/music/canon.ges` is a round handed to somebody else's
# synthesiser over MIDI.  This one carries its own instrument, so the piece
# and the sound are one program and `voices.consort` is checked against the
# notes it is given.

# ── What a note carries ──────────────────────────────────────────────────
#
# The key number and how hard.  A note's **timing is not in it**: that
# comes from the layout that placed it, or from the moment a key went down,
# and the bank supplies it separately as a `Sig Gate`.
#
# Not called `Note`: `music.ges` declares `Score a := Note a`, and `'x`
# desugars to that constructor — shadowing it would turn every `'` in the
# piece below into a pattern against the wrong thing.

Bowed := Bowed Int Int                      # key number, velocity

#: Twelve voices for five parts, and the factor of two is the **release**.
#: A note is let go at the end of its ticksPerBeat and goes on decaying for 0.35 s
#: into a ticksPerBeat that is 0.94 s long, so for a third of every ticksPerBeat the five
#: notes arriving overlap the five leaving.  A bank of eight would steal a
#: voice from a tail to give it to a new note, and a truncated release is a
#: click — the one artefact in a slow piece that is impossible to miss and
#: hard to attribute.
#:
#: The bank's name is bound to the **sum** of its voices, which is why
#: `sound` at the bottom is ordinary signal code.
voices consort 12 bowedVoice : Sig Float

#: How a MIDI note becomes this payload.  It says the payload *can* come
#: from a keyboard, not that this bank listens: which bank the keyboard
#: drives is a switch in the editor, beside the bank's own line.  Play
#: along with it.
instance FromMIDI Bowed where
    noteOn ch p v = Just (Bowed p v)

# ── The instrument ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────

#: Slow on and slow off, which is what makes a consort a consort: a fast
#: attack on five sustained parts turns a suspension into a click.
env : Adsr
env = Adsr 0.06 0.35 0.72 0.35

hzOf : Bowed -> Float
hzOf b = case b of
    Bowed k v -> keyHz k

levelOf : Bowed -> Float
levelOf b = case b of
    Bowed k v -> toFloat v / 127.0

#: The timbre: a fundamental and two harmonics over it, **added**.
#:
#: Additive synthesis is the one method that is nothing but arithmetic, so
#: it is the one that shows what `Num (Sig Float)` is worth — this is three
#: oscillators and two `zip`s, and it does not say so once.  Each
#: harmonic is quieter than the last, which is roughly what a bowed string
#: does and is the difference between a chord and a siren.
timbre : Sig Float -> Sig Float
timbre hz = sine hz + 0.5 * sine (hz * 2.0) + 0.3 * sine (hz * 3.0)

#: A number near 1.0 that wavers, for a hertz to be multiplied by.
#:
#: **A vibrato is a factor, not a feature.**  There is no vibrato in this
#: instrument and nothing implements one: there is a signal that hovers
#: around one, and multiplying a frequency by it is the whole mechanism.
#: `sine` is bipolar, so the note sits in the middle of the sweep rather
#: than at the bottom of it.
vibrato : Sig Float
vibrato = 1.0 + 0.004 * sine 5.0

#: The voice: three signals multiplied together, and the pitch is a product
#: too.  A `voices` bank hands each of its voices the note's timing and the
#: note's payload as two signals, so the envelope reads one and the pitch
#: reads the other and neither opens a record to find its half.
bowedVoice : Sig Gate -> Sig Bowed -> Sig Float
bowedVoice g s = timbre (!hzOf s * vibrato) * adsr env g * !levelOf s

# ── The piece ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
#
# 4/4, one note per ticksPerBeat throughout: the ground, the harmony above it and
# the tune above that all move together, note against note.  That is what
# makes the chords come out — F# over D, E over A, D over B, C# over F# —
# every tune note a tone of the chord its bass note is standing on.
#
# **This is the progression and the tune, not the canon proper.**  In
# Pachelbel the three upper parts are one line entered three times, two
# bars apart, and that only works because the line has *three distinct
# two-bar variations* before it repeats: with a single phrase and entries
# one ground-cycle apart, the three voices come out in unison and there is
# no canon at all.  Writing the variations is composition rather than an
# example, so what stands here is the harmony those parts spell out, which
# is the part everyone recognises anyway.
#
# **Five notes sound at once** — bass, three chord tones and the tune —
# which is the honest test that a `voices` bank is polyphonic.  A `||`
# starts everything inside it at the same instant, so a chord is three
# notes written side by side and needs nothing the language did not have.

#: Slow, which this piece is: the ground turns over in about seven seconds.
bpm : Int
bpm = 64

bar : Int
bar = 4 * ticksPerBeat

#: A ground note, and loudest — it is carrying the harmony and the parts
#: above it are thinner than it is.
low : Int -> [: Bowed :]
low k = '(Bowed k 100)

#: An inner part.  Quietest of the three: there are three of them at once
#: and they are filling the chord in rather than saying anything.
mid : Int -> [: Bowed :]
mid k = '(Bowed k 54)

#: The tune, on top and clear of the rest.
high : Int -> [: Bowed :]
high k = '(Bowed k 78)

#: Three notes at the same instant.  `||` is the whole mechanism.
chord : Int -> Int -> Int -> [: Bowed :]
chord a b c = mid a || mid b || mid c

#: The basso ostinato: D A B F# G D G A, a ticksPerBeat each, two bars round.
#: **Every chord in the piece is this line's doing** — the parts above it
#: spell the harmony out, they do not choose it.
ground : [: Bowed :]
ground = low 50 ++ low 45 ++ low 47 ++ low 42
      ++ low 43 ++ low 38 ++ low 43 ++ low 45

#: What the ground implies, voiced in the octave below middle C:
#: D, A, Bm, F#m, G, D, G, A.
harmony : [: Bowed :]
harmony = chord 62 66 69 ++ chord 57 61 64 ++ chord 59 62 66 ++ chord 54 57 61
       ++ chord 55 59 62 ++ chord 50 54 57 ++ chord 55 59 62 ++ chord 57 61 64

#: The tune: F# E D C# B A B C#, down the scale a step at a time and back
#: up two.  Eight notes against the ground's eight, one for one.
tune : [: Bowed :]
tune = high 78 ++ high 76 ++ high 74 ++ high 73
    ++ high 71 ++ high 69 ++ high 71 ++ high 73

twice : [: Bowed :] -> [: Bowed :]
twice s = s ++ s

thrice : [: Bowed :] -> [: Bowed :]
thrice s = s ++ s ++ s

#: Eight statements of the ground — sixteen bars, which is the length of
#: the piece.  `twice` three times, because a repeat is a function of a
#: score and not a number to be kept in step with anything.
bass : [: Bowed :]
bass = twice (twice (twice ground))

#: **The parts come in one at a time and leave the same way**, which is how
#: a ground-bass piece opens and is the clearest way to hear what each of
#: them is doing.  Two bars of bass alone, two more with the chords, the
#: tune from bar five; then the tune stops at bar thirteen, the chords at
#: fifteen, and the bass closes as it began — 1, 4, 5, 4, 1 parts.
#:
#: **None of that is written down as a length.**  `at` translates a score's
#: extent and leaves its duration alone, so each part is simply as long as
#: it is and starts where it starts: six statements of the harmony from bar
#: three and four of the tune from bar five happen to end where they do.
#: Every offset is measured from the same origin because `||` starts what
#: is inside it together, which is also what makes the arithmetic
#: checkable — see the note counts in the comment at the top of the piece.
upper : [: Bowed :]
upper = at (2 * bar) (twice (thrice harmony))
     || at (4 * bar) (twice (twice tune))

#: `voices.consort` names the bank **lexically**, so the type checker can
#: say that this piece fits this instrument.  Written apart, "do these
#: notes suit this bank" would be discovered as silence.  The result is
#: `[: Void :]`: every note committed, and nothing left unassigned.
score : [: Void :]
score = (bass || upper) >>= voices.consort

# ── The mix ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

#: 0.16, and set on the *thickest* bar rather than on one note: the bank's
#: name is the sum of its voices, so four parts are four times a single
#: note's amplitude and a level judged on the opening bass alone clips the
#: moment the third entry arrives.
#:
#: The lowpass is the room.  Three harmonics on four parts is a lot of
#: energy above 3 kHz, and taking the top off is what stops a consort of
#: sine sums from sounding like a pipe organ.
sound : Sig Float
sound = gain 0.16 (lowpassOnePole 2400.0 consort)
