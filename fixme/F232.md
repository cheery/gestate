# F232. **[resolved]** declared constructors counted straight through the reserved tags, and the seventy-seventh was `Nothing`

Found 2026-09-14 while building Q9 of `card:strict-forms.md`: five
constructors added to `gui.ges` (`Atom`, `Act`) put `facts.ges`'
`TyInt` and `TyTuple` on tags 80 and 81, and the stage refused every
`document` with

    the splice is a `Just`, not a `Type`

`declarations.classify` hands out tags from 4 upward, one per
constructor, and `gmachine` pins `Nothing`/`Just` at 80–81, `Sync` at
82–84, the FRP nodes at 90–96 and tuples from 200.  Nothing stepped
over the pinned ones, so a chain declaring seventy-seven or more
constructors had two named the same number and the machine could not
tell them apart.  Whether it showed depended on which two collided:
here it was a `TyTuple` read back as a `Just`; a program matching on
`Nothing` would have taken the arm for whichever user constructor
shared the tag.

**Resolved the same day.**  `fresh_tag` steps over every reserved tag,
and refuses at the tuple base with *too many constructors* rather than
counting into it.  `pipeline._STACK_SCHEMA` went to 4, because a
cached stack front bakes the numbering in.  Held by
`test/test_declarations.py::test_declared_constructors_never_take_a_reserved_tag`.
