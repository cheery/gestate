# F249. **[resolved]** where two sibling regions overlap, a press reaches the one painted underneath

**2026-10-03**, found building resize on the note hand
(`card:gui-is-difficult.md` §"The next slice: resize, on the note
hand").  `carry.ges` drew a note's bar and then its end over it,
`Over (hold (noteBar …)) (hold end (endOf n))`, and a press on the end's
inner 8 px was taken by the bar: a carry, not a resize.

`gui.py`'s `_under` takes the **first** hit containing the point, and
`_walk` records siblings in painter's order, so of two siblings the one
drawn first, which is underneath, wins.  For nesting that order is
right (the innermost is recorded first).  For siblings it is the
reverse of what a person sees.  `shell/panel/src/canvas.rs` keeps the
same rule, and the two are held to each other, so a fix is both
machines at once.

    # in carry.ges, swap the Over back — bar first, end over it — and
    python -m pytest test/test_reactors.py -k pulled_end    # red: a carry

**Not fixed the same day**: `carry.ges` drew the end first, as nothing.

**Resolved 2026-10-03**, because band select begins on the lane and the
lane is painted under the notes: the lane would have taken every press
on a note.  Both machines now take the deepest hit holding the point
and, of two siblings, the one painted later: a later hit holding the
point is a sibling on top when it does not enclose the one in hand, and
*around* it when it does.  `gui.py`'s `_under`, `list.rs`' `top`.  Held
by `test_gui.py`'s and `list.rs`'
`of_two_siblings_the_one_painted_later_takes_the_press`, each red under
the old rule.  `carry.ges` draws the end over the bar again.

**What a flat table cannot say:** a sibling on top that covers the one
beneath whole reads as its parent, so the one beneath still wins there.
Telling them apart needs the walk to record depth.
