# F245. **[missing]** a reactor block's refusal says its line in words, and the window does not draw it under the line

**2026-10-02**, with `gestate/reactors.py`.  Every `ReactorError` names
the author's line — *"line 52: `put other` in a reaction that declares
`at`…"* — but in prose, not in a form `audiospans` places, so the
complaint lands in the status line and not under the line it is about.
The refusal is raised in `notes.expanded`, before assembly, so its line
numbers are the author's file's and not the assembled program's — which
is why they were not written as `(at L:C)`: that form is translated
through the prelude's offset, and putting an author line through it
would place the mark on the wrong line, which is worse than none.

**Owed:** a position form for a pre-assembly complaint that the window
maps without an offset, or a `ReactorError` carrying its line as data.
`#: complaint  author, unplaced` in `reactors.py` points here.
