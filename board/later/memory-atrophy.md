## What this is, what it is not, when it runs

**It is a change of practice, adopted one move at a time.**  Four moves,
below, in order, each one small enough to land in a single fire and then
be left alone for a few of them.  Nothing is built.  There is no page to
write and no tool to run.

**It is not a memory-training programme**, and it is not a quiz, a
score, a streak or a recall rate.  A number on the author's memory would
be a number nobody asked for, and it would make the fire into a demand,
which `vision.md` forbids in as many words.  The refusal is part of the
card, not an omission from it.

**It runs at the fire** (moves 1 and 3), **in every conversation**
(move 2), and **whenever somebody is taught** (move 4).  Which is why it
is here rather than shelved: it costs a session almost nothing and it
costs the author minutes, spread thin, forever.

## Found by looking, 2026-08-21

**The tree carries three different kinds of thing, and only one of them
hurts to stop holding.**  This is the distinction the card turns on, and
it is the session's, not his:

1. **Facts nobody should hold** — dates, line counts, which shelf a card
   is on, F-numbers.  Offloading these is pure gain; the atrophy is
   real and does not matter.
2. **Structure** — the shape of gestate, what his own rules say, why a
   decision went the way it did.  Offloading this *feels* as free as (1)
   and is not.
3. **The conviction layer** — what it is for, which door, when to stop.
   `keeper.md` already says the tree cannot carry it.

**The risk is not the tree creeping into (3).  It is the tree taking
(2), which is what (3) runs on.**  Judgment cannot weigh material you
would have to look up, because you do not know to look it up.  The tree
could keep its promise about the five per cent exactly and still hollow
it out from underneath, and nothing in the suite would light.

**And the counterfactual is the other half of the honesty.**  Without
the tree he would not be remembering this material better — there would
be less of it, re-derived worse each week, and the journal exists
because half a megabyte of work happened.  The offloading is what bought
the scale.  So *write less down* is not a move here and does not appear
below.

**What could not be measured.**  There is no instance in the tree of him
failing to recall something he wrote — not because there is none, but
because nothing records that.  So the need is stated and not measured,
which `board/README.md` §"What the questioning has to do" asks be said
out loud rather than papered over.  Question 1 is that gap.

**The one mechanism with evidence behind it** is retrieval practice:
attempting recall *before* consulting, including when the attempt fails,
is what maintains access.  Re-reading does not.  Every move below is the
same shape — **recall first, then open the file** — and no move asks for
more reading.

## The four moves, in the order they should be taken

Each is written to be adoptable alone, and to survive being the only one
ever adopted.

### Move 1 — act 3 of the fire, closed book

`keeper.md` act 3 says *read it, then ask of the week*.  Invert it: write
what the rule says from memory, in a sentence, **then** open it.

Two findings for the price of one act: the gap is a reading on his
recall, and it is *also* a reading on the rule.  A rule he cannot
approximate from memory is a rule nobody could have followed, which is
already outcome three of that act — so this sharpens an existing
measurement rather than adding one.

Cost: zero extra minutes.  Session's work: a two-line edit to
`keeper.md`.  **Take this one first and let it run several fires before
move 2.**

### Move 2 — the session asks before it tells

When he asks something whose answer is **his** — the vision, a rule he
wrote, why a decision went the way it did — the session asks what he
remembers first, in one line, and then fills the gap.

**Narrowly scoped, or it is just friction.**  Not for paths, line
numbers, F-numbers, API shapes, or anything in layer (1) above; there
the lookup is the whole point of the tree.  And when a session does
fetch from the tree, it names *where it lives*, so the index stays in
his head even when the content does not.

This is the only move that changes how a session behaves, and it makes
the session deliberately slower on a narrow class of question.  **It
needs his consent** — question 2.

### Move 3 — the cap gets its second reason

`spec/rules.md` §"Why there is a cap at all" gives one reason: a session
reads all five documents every shift.  There is a second, and it is this
card's: **a method he could recite is worth more than a longer one he
can search.**  The 2,000-line cap and `vision.md`'s deliberate shortness
are already atrophy controls; they are just not documented as such.

One line, in that section.  Changes no mechanism — it stops the cap
looking like it is only about sessions, so that the next person tempted
to raise it knows both things it is holding.

### Move 4 — teaching counts double, and is already on the board

Explaining without notes is retrieval under load, the strongest exercise
there is.  `card:stranger-test.md`, `card:git-lesson.md`, and
`doc/method.md` as the visitors' front are all this, already, and fire on
pull rather than on schedule.

**Nothing to build and nothing to adopt.**  It is here so that when one
of them comes up it is not read as pure cost, and so that this card does
not invent a fifth mechanism for a job three existing ones already do.

## The postcondition

**A year from now, asked about something he wrote, he answers from
memory first and the tree confirms him — rather than the tree answering
and him recognising it.**

Derived from the `because`, names no function, and is checkable only by
him.  That is the honest shape here: the thing this card is about does
not have an instrument, and building one would be the failure mode the
card already refuses.

## What would make this card wrong

*Written before the moves are taken, per `board/README.md`
§"The postcondition, before anything is built" — the rehearsal is worth
nothing without something written down that would change.*

* **If what he is losing is layer (1).**  Then the card solves a
  non-problem and costs him minutes at every fire.  The tell: he can
  still *decide* without lookup, and only the recitation has gone.
  Retire the card; keep move 3.
* **If the fire gets heavier and he skips fires.**  Then this has cost
  more than it bought, because the fire is the thing that carries four
  other acts.  `keeper.md` — *a missed evening is not a red gate*.  The
  tell is skipped fires, not a complaint.  Revert move 1 first.
* **If move 2 runs ten times and never finds a gap.**  Then it is
  friction wearing the costume of a practice.  Drop it and say so here;
  a finding that a defence was unnecessary is a finding.
* **If any of this acquires a number.**  A score, a streak, a percentage
  — that is the card having become the thing it was written to refuse.
