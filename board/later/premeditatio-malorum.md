## Found by looking, before it was taken

**The practice is already here, four times, and never by name.**  That
is what makes this a lesson card rather than a build item — nothing has
to be built, and something has to be *seen*.

* **Set-based work** — several answers with what would kill each.  A
  plan is not proposed here without its own failure rehearsed first;
  that is `premeditatio malorum` applied to a decision, and it is
  `doc/memory/henri-working-style.md`'s oldest rule.
* **`manifesto.md` §"The three ways an instrument fails"** — every
  instrument in this tree is required to carry the account of how it
  will lie, written while it is being built rather than after it has.
* **`board/README.md` §"The postcondition, before anything is built"** —
  say what would show the work succeeded, before doing it, when it is
  still possible to be honest about it.
* **`spec/rocks.md`** — the 6.5 MB gif.  A cost made visible *at the
  moment the person could act on it*, rather than found days later in a
  stranger's slow clone.

**And the blast-radius reading of the fence is the fifth**, added
2026-08-20: sizing what a mistake would cost before one happens, rather
than counting incidents afterwards — `doc/memory/gestate-hardening.md`.

## What the lesson would have to cover

*Suspected — this is the session's sketch and it has not been taught to
anybody yet.*

**The Stoic version, briefly and accurately**, because the name carries
its own baggage: Seneca's rehearsal of loss, and Marcus writing the
morning list of who he will meet.  It is **not** pessimism and not
worry.  Worry loops on an outcome; premeditation *finishes* with it, and
the finishing is what buys the calm.

**Where it stops being philosophy and becomes engineering**: the
practice fails in one predictable way — rehearsing what would go wrong
and then not changing anything.  A premeditation with no consequence is
a mood, which is `manifesto.md`'s complaint about unmeasured claims
wearing another hat.  Each instance above answers that by attaching the
rehearsal to something that has to be *written down*: a kill-condition,
a failure mode, a postcondition, a number.

**And its pair, which Henri already supplied on 2026-08-20**:
*amor fati*.  Rehearse the loss, then affirm what comes anyway.  The two
are one practice — the rehearsal without the affirmation is dread, and
the affirmation without the rehearsal is bravado.  `doc/memory/gestate-hardening.md`
carries his use of the second half, on trust.

**2. Does naming a practice already being followed change anything?**
Worth answering honestly rather than assuming yes.  The argument for:
four rules that are one idea can drift apart, and nothing in the suite
compares them — the same defect `card:working-standard.md` found in the
two-writers rule stated three times.  The argument against: a name that
is only a name adds a line to a rules set with 15 to spare.

**3. What is it for — Henri, or a session?**  The ask is *teach me*, so
the primary reader is him.  But three of the four instances are rules a
session follows, so a lesson that only reaches him leaves the practice
un-named where it is actually performed.
